Performance

Ecdysis

2017

Video Recorded Performance of Unveiling layers of material.

Each layers represent a characteristic of the projected identity assigned socially to an individual.

The stain on face being the flawed version of the perfectly inward-projected identity of oneself, the performance ends with a small breathing area, partially and imperfectly escaping the identity projection, yet still actively wearing the persona.

Pulse

2017

Performance of shattering a painted mirror.

Shock and Adventure

2013

Photographed series of Performance which explores the area of shock and adventure within different location and social settings.
