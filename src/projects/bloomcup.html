Bloom Cup

Medium: 3D printed body and components, carved pine wood handle, laser cut acrylic wings, sound sensors, servos, custom circuit protoboard, painted button

Dimensions: 4 × 2 × 8 in (W × D × H)

Exploring absurdity and playful solutions to everyday problems, this experimental device is designed to prevent accidental spit from entering a cup during conversation. The cup detects sound levels in its surroundings, and when a threshold is exceeded, mechanical wings flap upward to shield the drink from airborne droplets.

The system integrates sound sensors, servos, and a custom circuit with battery power and a charging port. A carved wooden handle and minimal white body with a green accent were designed to balance the object’s humorous function with a clean, product inspired aesthetic.

*This project was exhibited at the Interactive Media Show, Brooklyn, 2025.
