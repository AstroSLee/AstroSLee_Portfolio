A work in progress project.

The Heart Symphony is a real time project which uses the input from Pulse Sensor to Arduino to Touch Designer to create visual and sound feedback. The heart signal of the participant becomes a part of a composed song, with multiple participants inputting heart signals, creating a symphony as a collected and connected body.

(Click the sound on for the audio feedback - on the website not real-time, but you'll get the idea.) There's multiple versions of the audio that are also a work in progress.
